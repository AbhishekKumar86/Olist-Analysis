# Olist E-commerce Dataset Analysis

# 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the Brazilian e-commerce dataset (Olist) using SQL. The goal is to extract customer, seller, and business insights from orders, payments, reviews, and product categories to support better decision-making.

## Dataset & Source

- Downalod the Dataset from Kaggle Websie.
- Customers-customer profiles (state, city, unique ID)
- Orders – order details with timestamps
- Order_Items – product, seller, and pricing per order
- Products – product catalog and categories
- Sellers – seller details (state, city)
- Order_Payments – payment method and amount
- Order_Review – customer ratings and review scores
- Product_Category – category mapping for products

## Data Modeling & Schema Design

- Customer → customer_id as primary key
- Orders → linked to customers via customer_id
- Order_Items → connects orders to sellers and products (order_id, product_id, seller_id)
- Products → product_id as primary key, linked to categories
- Order_Review → foreign key order_id linked to Orders
- Order_Review → foreign key order_id linked to Orders

## Key Insights
### Customer Experience
- Average review score is 4, showing generally positive customer satisfaction.
- Certain sellers have consistently low ratings (<3).
- Product categories like furniture, electronics, and appliances generate the most 1 star reviews.
- Reviews: 10–12% are 1-star, while 50%+ are 5-star.

### Revenue & Payments
- Credit Card has done the highest revenue (12.54million (approx.), followed by boleto (bank slip) (2.87million (approx.).
- São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais are top contributors to total revenue.
- Average order value per customer is ($6k-10)(approx).

### Seller Performance

## Business Recommendations
- Provide seller training programs (packaging, communication, handling complaints).
- Implement a loyalty program for top customers.
- Target product categories with frequent poor reviews for stricter monitoring.
- Mnitoring and Checking that sellers meet certain standards before their products and services are delivered to customers.
- Giving Special benefits to customers who pay via debit or boleto to increase adoption.
